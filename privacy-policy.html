1. Information You Enter

SmartBudget allows you to record budgeting and expense information. This may include expense titles, amounts, categories, dates, and your monthly budget amount.

2. Local Storage

The currently reviewed app implementation stores expense records and the monthly budget locally using device app preferences. The reviewed application code does not show a SmartBudget-operated server or account system. The complete release project and its included libraries must be checked before this statement is finalized.

3. Exporting and Sharing Files

SmartBudget can create CSV exports and JSON backup files. These files may contain your budget and expense information.

If you choose to save or share an exported file, the destination app or service you select may process that file under its own privacy practices. You are responsible for choosing a trusted destination and protecting exported files.

4. Restoring Backups

You can select a JSON backup file to restore information. Restoring a backup may replace the budget and expense information currently stored in SmartBudget. Keep a separate copy of important information before restoring a backup.

5. Collection and Sharing

We do not intend to receive your entered budget and expense information through a SmartBudget-operated server. However, the handling of information by operating-system services, file-selection and sharing features, and third-party libraries must be verified for the released version.

If the application's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.
